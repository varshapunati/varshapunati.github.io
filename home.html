about me

Hello there, I’m Varsha Punati. I would say I am and always have been a creative. I find beauty in the mundane, and my creative avenues stretch from graphic design to Indian classical dance to photography.


Graphic design is ones of my few high risk, high reward hobbies, and with my time at CSU Monterey Bay, I have been lucky enough to expand that into a career. Through hours of experimental passion and with the kindness of my many professors I have had the ability to hone my skills across various design applications in the Adobe Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign, as well as Figma).


Coming out of college I have felt a renewed sense of appreciation for design as a whole. It subconsciously shapes how we interact with the and I think that is beyond powerful.
